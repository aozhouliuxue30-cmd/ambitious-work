# Example: Improve a resume using real experience

[中文](resume-review.md) | [English](resume-review.en.md)

Version: 1.1.0

These are fictional teaching materials. When substituting your own inputs, preserve real responsibilities and completion status; do not invent metrics.

## Copyable input

```text
Output language: [中文 / English / 中文 + English]
Use Ambitious Work to complete a ready-to-use resume project entry.
Give the revision first, then matches to the role and information still needed.

Target role requirements: Organize requirements, make interface prototypes,
communicate with developers, and explain design rationale.
Existing experience:
March to May 2026, campus club event registration project.
I helped organize registration requirements supplied by the organizer and made
prototypes for the registration and submission-success pages. In two discussions,
I recorded implementation constraints raised by a developer teammate and revised
the relevant pages. Three people collaborated on the project. I did not develop
the product and have no usage data after launch.

Constraints: At most three resume bullets. Do not invent user growth, efficiency gains,
or test outcomes. Do not describe the three-person project as my solo work or turn
“helped organize requirements” into “led requirements.”
Acceptance criteria: Every bullet has a clear action and output. State actual gaps
separately; do not put unsupported job requirements into the experience entry.
```

## Expected output structure

1. **Replacement experience entry:** Project, dates, collaboration context, and no more than three bullets.
2. **Matches and gaps:** Job requirements, available evidence, and evidence still needed.
3. **Fact check:** Confirm that no metrics, launch status, or responsibilities were added.

## Acceptable reference excerpt

> Campus club event registration project | Mar–May 2026 | Three-person collaboration
>
> - Helped organize the event organizer's registration requirements and created prototypes for the registration and submission-success pages.
> - Recorded a developer teammate's implementation constraints in two discussions and revised the relevant pages accordingly.
>
> Missing information: The materials do not explain specific design rationale or show post-launch results. Add real records of design decisions if available; do not claim validated impact without evidence.

## What to check

- “Helped” and “three-person collaboration” remain visible.
- No unsupported “led,” “developed independently,” or “improved by X%.”
- “Design rationale was not provided” does not become “no design judgment ever occurred.”
- Missing information stays outside the final experience entry.
- In bilingual output, preserve dates, the two discussions, responsibilities, and team boundaries in both languages.

This reference structure and excerpt were authored as acceptance guidance with AI assistance. They are not outputs from a completed model test.
