# Ambitious Work · Copyable prompts

[中文](PROMPTS.md) | [English](PROMPTS.en.md)

Version: 1.1.0. No installation is needed; replace the bracketed fields. You can also use the [offline bilingual tool](docs/index.html) to generate, copy, or download a prompt. The tool does not call a model, upload inputs, or require a key. Execution depends on your AI product.

Follow an explicit Chinese, English, or bilingual request. Otherwise follow the user's message language. Pair bilingual paragraphs and preserve numbers, citations, facts, and uncertainty. Interface and deliverable languages are independent.

## General entry point

```text
Use the Ambitious Work workflow to complete the task below.

Task: [The specific deliverable and who will use it]
Materials: [Paste sources, or attach files and identify what to read]
Constraints: [Facts, scope, format, and actions that must be preserved or avoided]
Acceptance criteria: [How to check whether the deliverable is usable]
Output language: [中文 / English / 中文 + English]

Read the supplied materials first. Separate facts, assumptions, and missing information.
When the goal is clear, start work and produce the first version possible with the
available inputs; do not stop at a plan.
Preserve my original requirements. Do not invent experience, data, citations, or test results.
Check the first version against the acceptance criteria and fix what can be fixed.
Deliver the artifact first, then briefly state what was actually checked and what remains unverified.
Follow an explicit language choice; otherwise follow the language of my message.
For bilingual output, pair Chinese and English paragraph by paragraph, keeping numbers,
citations, facts, and uncertainty consistent. Interface language and deliverable language
are independent; the output language selected here controls the deliverable.
If necessary tools or materials are missing, explain the gap and complete the work that
does not depend on it.
For external publishing, sending, payments, or deletion, follow my explicit authorization
and the host tool's confirmation rules.
```

## Project brief

```text
Turn the following project materials into a usable project brief:
[Paste numbered project notes or feedback]

Audience: [Readers]
Length and format: [Requirements]
Output language: [中文 / English / 中文 + English]
Constraint: Use only the supplied materials. Do not invent participants, data, or causality.

Deliver the brief first, including at least: the problem, evidence with source labels,
alternative explanations, a recommended next step, and assumptions still to test.
Finally, check the evidence for each central claim and state what the materials cannot establish.
```

## Resume and job fit

```text
Target job requirements:
[Paste requirements]

My real experience:
[Paste experience]

Output language: [中文 / English / 中文 + English]
Give me a ready-to-use resume revision first, followed by matches and actual gaps.
Preserve my responsibilities, dates, and completion status. If metrics are missing,
use specific actions and outputs. Do not invent impact or turn team results into individual results.
Mark missing information separately; do not put placeholders for unverified claims into the final experience.
```

## A manageable experiment

```text
I want to try: [Direction]
My starting point and materials: [Actual situation]
Time and cost available: [Limits]
Deliverable I want: [First version]
Output language: [中文 / English / 中文 + English]

Design and complete the experiment preparation possible with the current inputs.
Deliver a concrete first version with inputs, actions, acceptance criteria, and stopping conditions.
Keep ambitious goals as hypotheses. Without an actual run or feedback, state that they remain unverified.
Keep the first step small. Do not register accounts, install software, pay, or publish without authorization.
```
